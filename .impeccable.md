## Design Context

### Users

Video editors and motion designers who already work confidently in Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects but should not need technical knowledge to reach media stored on a Feiniu NAS. Their job is to find a usable asset, inspect it quickly, and place it in the current edit without leaving the Adobe workspace.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, capable, and production-minded. The interface should feel dependable under deadline pressure, using Seek green only for meaningful state and primary actions.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dense Adobe-style dark utility panel rather than a website or marketing surface. It should visually belong beside the Project and Timeline panels, with stable thumbnail geometry, compact controls, and restrained borders. No decorative gradients, oversized headings, floating sections, or card-within-card layouts.

### Design Principles

- Keep the primary path obvious: choose folder, find asset, import or place.
- Prefer editing speed and scanability over explanation.
- Preserve context in narrow docked panels and wider floating panels.
- Keep browsing non-destructive by default. Rename and move-to-trash actions must be explicit, confirmed, recoverable where the filesystem supports it, and clearly warn about offline Adobe references.
- Use precise progress, empty, offline, and error states instead of silent failure.
