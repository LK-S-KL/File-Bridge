## Design Context

### Users

Video editors and motion designers who already work confidently in Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects but should not need technical knowledge to reach media stored on a Feiniu NAS. Their job is to find a usable asset, inspect it quickly, and place it in the current edit without leaving the Adobe workspace.

### Brand Personality

Compact, tactile, and production-minded. The interface should feel quick under deadline pressure, using vivid green only for selection and primary actions, with restrained warm red/orange accents for identity.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dense Adobe-style dark utility panel with near-black graphite modules, generous rounded corners, and icon-first controls inspired by high-end motion-tool panels. Selected items become unmistakably green and reveal a compact action/detail strip above the asset grid. Warm coral/orange is reserved for the LK identity; the browsing surface remains calm. No oversized headings, excessive glow, or persistent bottom inspector strip.

### Design Principles

- Keep the primary path obvious: choose folder, find asset, import or place.
- Prefer editing speed and scanability over explanation.
- Preserve context in narrow docked panels and wider floating panels.
- Reveal secondary actions progressively through selection and hover instead of permanently occupying the bottom of the panel.
- Keep browsing non-destructive by default. Rename and move-to-trash actions must be explicit, confirmed, recoverable where the filesystem supports it, and clearly warn about offline Adobe references.
- Use precise progress, empty, offline, and error states instead of silent failure.
